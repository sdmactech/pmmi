Conference Planning Timeline

A comprehensive guide to planning a successful scientific conference.

📅 18-24 Months Before

  • Form organizing committee
  • Define conference goals and objectives
  • Determine conference dates
  • Begin site selection process
  • Develop preliminary budget
  • Consider conference management company

📅 12-18 Months Before

  • Finalize venue selection and contracts
  • Establish program committee
  • Develop sponsorship packages
  • Create conference website
  • Begin marketing and promotion
  • Set up abstract submission system
  • Secure keynote speakers

📅 9-12 Months Before

  • Launch call for papers/abstracts
  • Open registration
  • Recruit exhibitors and sponsors
  • Book hotel room blocks
  • Plan social events
  • Coordinate with publishers (if applicable)

📅 6-9 Months Before

  • Abstract submission deadline
  • Coordinate peer review process
  • Finalize program schedule
  • Continue marketing efforts
  • Confirm AV and technology needs
  • Plan poster sessions

📅 3-6 Months Before

  • Send acceptance notifications
  • Finalize exhibitor floor plan
  • Produce program book
  • Coordinate catering and meals
  • Arrange transportation
  • Prepare registration materials

📅 1-3 Months Before

  • Final registration push
  • Confirm all speakers and presenters
  • Finalize room setups
  • Prepare signage
  • Brief staff and volunteers
  • Send pre-conference communications

📅 Conference Week

  • Set up registration area
  • Conduct staff briefings
  • Manage on-site registration
  • Coordinate sessions and speakers
  • Support exhibitors
  • Handle issues as they arise
  • Gather feedback

📅 Post-Conference

  • Send thank you communications
  • Distribute post-conference survey
  • Complete financial reconciliation
  • Prepare final report
  • Coordinate proceedings publication
  • Archive materials and data
  • Begin planning for next event

Conference Planning Best Practices

Key principles for successful scientific conferences.

🎯

Clear Objectives

Define clear goals and success metrics from the start. What do you want attendees to gain? How will you measure success?

💰

Realistic Budgeting

Develop comprehensive budgets with contingencies. Track expenses carefully and adjust as needed throughout the planning process.

📢

Early Marketing

Start promoting your conference early and consistently. Use multiple channels to reach your target audience.

🤝

Stakeholder Engagement

Maintain regular communication with all stakeholders including committee members, speakers, sponsors, and attendees.

⚙️

Technology Integration

Leverage technology for registration, abstract management, and attendee engagement. Choose user-friendly systems.

📋

Detailed Planning

Create comprehensive timelines and checklists. Document processes and decisions for future reference.

Common Conference Planning Challenges

And how to overcome them.

Challenge: Limited Time and Resources

Solution: Consider partnering with a professional conference management company. This allows your organizing committee to focus on scientific content while experts handle logistics.

Challenge: Low Registration Numbers

Solution: Start marketing early, offer early bird discounts, leverage social media, and engage your community. Ensure your value proposition is clear and compelling.

Challenge: Budget Overruns

Solution: Build contingencies into your budget (typically 10-15%), track expenses carefully, and make adjustments early when issues arise. Negotiate favorable contracts with vendors.

Challenge: Abstract/Paper Quality

Solution: Establish clear submission guidelines, implement rigorous peer review processes, and provide feedback to authors. Set high standards from the beginning.

Challenge: Technology Issues

Solution: Test all systems thoroughly before the conference, have backup plans, and ensure technical support is available on-site. Choose reliable, proven platforms.

Challenge: International Logistics

Solution: Work with experienced partners who understand international conference requirements, including visa support, currency considerations, and cultural differences.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should we start planning our conference?

Ideally, begin planning 18-24 months before your conference date. This allows adequate time for venue selection, speaker recruitment, and marketing. However, successful conferences can be planned in shorter timeframes with experienced management.

What are the benefits of hiring a conference management company?

Professional conference managers bring expertise, established vendor relationships, and proven systems. They handle time-consuming logistics, allowing your organizing committee to focus on scientific content. They also provide continuity and institutional knowledge for recurring conferences.

How do we select the right venue?

Consider factors including capacity, location accessibility, meeting space configuration, technology capabilities, accommodation options, and cost. Visit potential venues in person and negotiate favorable terms.

What technology do we need for abstract management?

Look for systems that support online submissions, peer review workflows, author communications, and program scheduling. The system should be user-friendly for both authors and reviewers, with robust reporting capabilities.

How do we handle conference proceedings?

Work with established publishers (such as IEEE, IOP, or others) who specialize in conference proceedings. Ensure clear author guidelines, rigorous peer review, and adherence to publisher requirements and deadlines.

What insurance do we need for our conference?

Consider general liability insurance, event cancellation insurance, and professional liability coverage. Requirements vary by venue and location. Consult with insurance professionals familiar with conference events.

How can we make our conference more sustainable?

Reduce printed materials, choose venues with sustainability certifications, offer digital alternatives, minimize food waste, encourage public transportation, and consider carbon offset programs.
