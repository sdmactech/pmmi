Our Services

Comprehensive conference management solutions tailored to the needs of scientific and technical research communities.

From strategic planning to post-event reporting, we provide end-to-end conference management services that allow you to focus on the science while we handle the logistics.

📋

Full Conference Management

Complete end-to-end conference planning and execution services.

Strategic Planning & Consultation

We work with your organizing committee to develop a comprehensive conference strategy, including goals, timelines, and success metrics. Our experience with similar events provides valuable insights and best practices.

Site Selection & Contract Negotiation

Leveraging our extensive network and negotiation expertise, we identify ideal venues and secure favorable contracts. We handle all venue communications and ensure your requirements are met.

Budget Development & Financial Management

We create detailed budgets, track expenses, manage revenue, and provide regular financial reports. Our financial oversight ensures your conference stays on budget and maximizes ROI.

Timeline & Project Management

We develop comprehensive project timelines with clear milestones and deliverables. Our project management approach ensures all tasks are completed on schedule.

Risk Management & Contingency Planning

We identify potential risks and develop contingency plans to address them. Our proactive approach minimizes disruptions and ensures smooth execution.

📝

Technical Program Management

Expert coordination of scientific programs and peer review processes.

Abstract Submission System Setup

We configure and manage sophisticated abstract submission platforms tailored to your conference requirements. Our systems support multiple submission types, custom fields, and automated workflows.

Peer Review Coordination

We facilitate the entire peer review process, from reviewer assignment to final decisions. Our experience with scientific review processes ensures quality and efficiency.

Program Committee Liaison

We serve as the primary point of contact for program committee members, handling communications, scheduling, and administrative support throughout the program development process.

Author Communications

We manage all author correspondence, including acceptance notifications, presentation guidelines, and deadline reminders. Our dedicated email systems ensure timely and professional communications.

Session Scheduling & Organization

We create optimized session schedules that maximize attendee engagement and minimize conflicts. Our scheduling expertise ensures smooth program flow.

Speaker Management

We coordinate with keynote speakers and session chairs, managing invitations, travel arrangements, and on-site support to ensure their presentations are successful.

Program Book Development

We produce comprehensive program books in both print and digital formats, including abstracts, schedules, author indices, and conference information.

🎫

Registration Services

Comprehensive registration management from setup to on-site support.

Online Registration System Setup

We configure user-friendly registration platforms with custom registration types, pricing tiers, and add-on options. Our systems support early bird pricing, group discounts, and complex registration scenarios.

Payment Processing

We handle secure payment processing with multiple payment options including credit cards, wire transfers, and purchase orders. Our systems comply with PCI standards and international payment requirements.

Attendee Database Management

We maintain comprehensive attendee databases with detailed information for reporting, communications, and future event planning. Our systems ensure data security and privacy.

Badge Production

We design and produce professional name badges with custom layouts, colors, and information. Our on-site badge printing capabilities accommodate last-minute registrations.

Registration Desk Operations

Our experienced staff manages on-site registration desks, handling check-ins, badge distribution, and attendee inquiries with professionalism and efficiency.

Attendee Communications

We manage all pre-conference and post-conference communications, including confirmation emails, reminders, and thank you messages. Our automated systems ensure timely delivery.

🎪

On-Site Conference Management

Professional on-site coordination ensuring flawless execution.

Registration Desk Operations

Our team manages all registration desk activities, from setup to breakdown. We handle attendee check-ins, badge distribution, and provide friendly, professional service throughout the conference.

Audio-Visual Coordination

We coordinate with AV vendors to ensure all technical requirements are met. Our team manages equipment setup, testing, and troubleshooting for presentations and sessions.

Room Setup & Logistics

We oversee all room setups, including seating arrangements, signage, materials distribution, and equipment placement. Our attention to detail ensures every session space is properly prepared.

Session Monitoring

Our staff monitors sessions to ensure they run on schedule, speakers have what they need, and any issues are quickly addressed. We maintain smooth transitions between presentations.

Vendor & Exhibitor Support

We provide on-site support for exhibitors and vendors, managing setup, addressing concerns, and ensuring their experience is positive and productive.

Problem-Solving & Troubleshooting

Our experienced team anticipates and quickly resolves issues as they arise. We maintain calm under pressure and ensure minimal disruption to your conference.

Staff Coordination

We manage all on-site staff, including volunteers, ensuring everyone understands their roles and responsibilities. Our team approach ensures comprehensive coverage.

Exhibit & Sponsorship Management

Complete exhibitor and sponsorship coordination services.

Exhibitor Recruitment

We develop and execute exhibitor recruitment strategies, reaching out to potential exhibitors and promoting the value of participating in your conference.

Booth Sales & Management

We handle all aspects of booth sales, from pricing and contracts to payment processing and exhibitor communications. Our systems streamline the entire process.

Sponsorship Package Development

We create attractive sponsorship packages that provide value to sponsors while generating revenue for your conference. Our packages are customizable and competitive.

Exhibitor Communications

We manage all exhibitor correspondence, providing timely information about setup, regulations, and opportunities. Our dedicated support ensures exhibitor satisfaction.

Floor Plan Design

We design efficient exhibit hall layouts that maximize space utilization and traffic flow. Our floor plans consider booth sizes, aisles, and special requirements.

On-Site Exhibitor Support

Our team provides comprehensive on-site support for exhibitors, managing setup, addressing concerns, and ensuring a positive experience that encourages future participation.

Publications Management

Expert coordination of conference proceedings and scientific publications.

Publisher Liaison

We coordinate with publishers (including IEEE, IOP, and others) to ensure conference proceedings meet all requirements and deadlines. Our experience with major publishers streamlines the process.

Submission System Management

We configure and manage paper submission systems with author templates, formatting guidelines, and automated workflows. Our systems support various submission types and requirements.

Author Template Development

We create and distribute author templates that comply with publisher requirements and ensure consistent formatting across all submissions.

Peer Review Coordination

We facilitate the peer review process for publications, managing reviewer assignments, tracking reviews, and coordinating with editors to ensure quality and timeliness.

Editorial Support

We provide comprehensive support to editorial committees, handling administrative tasks so editors can focus on scientific content and quality.

Conference Proceedings Production

We coordinate the production of conference proceedings, including front matter, table of contents, author indices, and final compilation. Our quality control ensures professional results.

Marketing & Communications

Strategic marketing and communication services to promote your conference.

Conference Website Development

We design and develop professional conference websites with all essential information, registration integration, and user-friendly navigation. Our sites are mobile-responsive and optimized for search engines.

Email Marketing Campaigns

Social Media Management

We manage social media presence for your conference, creating engaging content and building community engagement before, during, and after the event.

Promotional Materials

We design and produce promotional materials including flyers, posters, brochures, and digital assets that effectively communicate your conference value proposition.

Attendee Communications

We manage comprehensive communication strategies to keep attendees informed and engaged throughout the conference lifecycle.

Post-Conference Surveys

We design and distribute post-conference surveys to gather feedback, measure satisfaction, and identify areas for improvement in future events.
