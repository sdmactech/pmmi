MEMS

MEMS Conference Series

🏛️ IEEE Sponsored 🌍 Global Locations 📅 Annual Event

The IEEE International Conference on Micro Electro Mechanical Systems (MEMS) is the premier international conference for MEMS researchers and engineers. We have proudly managed multiple MEMS conferences across the globe, including events in Taiwan, Belfast, Austin, Miami, and other international locations.

Our comprehensive management includes abstract submission systems, peer review coordination, registration management, on-site logistics, and IEEE proceedings publication support.

Recent Conferences:
  • MEMS 2025 - Kaohsiung, Taiwan (January 19-23, 2025)
  • MEMS 2024 - Austin, Texas, USA
  • MEMS 2018 - Belfast, Northern Ireland
  • MEMS 2005 - Miami, Florida, USA
T25

Transducers Conference Series

🔬 Sensors & Actuators 🌐 International 📅 Biennial Event

Transducers is the world's premier conference on solid-state sensors, actuators, and microsystems. This flagship event brings together researchers, engineers, and industry leaders from around the world to share the latest advances in transducer technology.

We provide full conference management services including technical program coordination, abstract management, registration systems, exhibit management, and comprehensive on-site support.

Upcoming Conference:
  • Transducers 2025 - Orlando, Florida, USA (June 29 - July 3, 2025)
μTAS

MicroTAS Conference Series

🧪 Lab-on-Chip 🌍 Global Event 📅 Annual Conference

The International Conference on Miniaturized Systems for Chemistry and Life Sciences (MicroTAS) is the world's leading conference on micro total analysis systems and lab-on-chip technologies. The conference covers all aspects of miniaturized systems for chemical and biological analysis.

Our services include complete conference management from abstract submission through post-conference proceedings publication, working closely with the Chemical and Biological Microsystems Society.

Recent Conferences:
  • MicroTAS 2025 - Upcoming
  • MicroTAS 2024 - Montréal, Canada (October 13-17, 2024)
  • MicroTAS 2020 - Virtual Conference
  • MicroTAS 2016 - Dublin, Ireland
P+

PowerMEMS+ Conference Series

⚡ Energy Harvesting 🏛️ IEEE Sponsored 📅 Annual Workshop

PowerMEMS+ is the international workshop on micro and nanotechnology for power generation and energy conversion applications. The conference focuses on power MEMS, energy harvesting, and related technologies for autonomous systems.

We provide comprehensive conference management including technical program coordination, registration systems, exhibit management, and IEEE proceedings publication support.

Upcoming Conference:
  • PowerMEMS+ 2025 - Albuquerque, New Mexico, USA (December 15-18, 2025)
  • PowerMEMS 2024 - Previous Event
  • PowerMEMS 2009 - Washington DC, USA
CBMS

Chemical and Biological Microsystems Society

🔬 Research Society 🌐 Global Network 📅 Ongoing Support

The Chemical and Biological Microsystems Society (CBMS) is dedicated to advancing the field of chemical and biological microsystems. We serve as the society's conference management partner, providing comprehensive administrative and logistical support.

Our services include society administration, conference management, member communications, and coordination with related events and organizations in the microsystems community.

μFIP

MicroFIP Conference

💡 Microfluidics & Photonics 🌐 International 📅 Biennial Event

MicroFIP is the international conference on microfluidics and integrated photonics, bringing together researchers working at the intersection of these two cutting-edge technologies. The conference explores applications in sensing, communications, and biomedical devices.

We provide full conference management services including program coordination, registration, and on-site logistics for this specialized technical conference.

Upcoming Conference:
  • MicroFIP 2025 - Santa Barbara, California, USA (June 16-18, 2025)
