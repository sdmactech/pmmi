## Design Considerations

### Color Scheme
- Professional blues and grays (trust, stability)
- Accent color for CTAs (orange or green for action)
- White space for clarity
- Consistent throughout site

### Typography
- Clean, modern sans-serif for body text
- Professional serif or bold sans-serif for headings
- Readable font sizes (16px minimum for body)
- Proper line spacing

### Imagery
- Professional conference photos
- International locations
- Diverse attendees
- Technology and innovation themes
- High-quality, professional photography

### Layout
- Clean, modern design
- Grid-based layouts
- Consistent spacing
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Responsive breakpoints

## Conversion Optimization

### Forms
- Keep forms short and simple
- Clear labels and instructions
- Inline validation
- Thank you page after submission
- Auto-responder emails

### Trust Signals
- Client logos (with permission)
- Testimonials throughout site
- Years in business
- Number of conferences managed
- Professional certifications
- Security badges

### User Experience
- Fast loading times (under 3 seconds)
- Clear navigation
- Breadcrumbs on deep pages
- Search functionality
- Easy-to-find contact information
- Mobile-friendly design
