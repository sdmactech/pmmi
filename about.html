About Preferred Meeting Management

Decades of expertise in scientific conference management, serving the world's leading research communities.

Our Story

Preferred Meeting Management, Inc. has been the trusted partner for scientific and technical conference management for over 25 years.

Based in San Diego, California, we specialize in providing comprehensive conference management services to the MEMS, microsystems, and technical research communities. Our deep understanding of scientific conference requirements, combined with our commitment to excellence, has made us the preferred choice for prestigious international conferences and research societies.

We pride ourselves on our long-term partnerships with conference organizers and research societies. Many of our clients have worked with us for decades, trusting us to manage their most important events year after year. This continuity allows us to provide unmatched institutional knowledge and seamless execution.

Our team brings together expertise in event management, scientific publishing, IEEE standards, and international logistics. We understand the unique challenges of managing technical conferences, from coordinating peer-reviewed paper submissions to ensuring flawless on-site execution across multiple continents.
