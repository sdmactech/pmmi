# Preferred Meeting Management Website

A professional website for Preferred Meeting Management, Inc. - a San Diego-based scientific conference management company specializing in MEMS, microsystems, and technical research conferences.

## Overview

This website showcases the company's expertise in managing prestigious international scientific conferences including MEMS, Transducers, MicroTAS, PowerMEMS+, and other technical research events.

## Features

- **Responsive Design**: Fully responsive layout that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Modern UI**: Clean, professional design with smooth animations and transitions
- **Comprehensive Content**: Detailed information about services, portfolio, and resources
- **Contact Forms**: Interactive contact form with validation
- **SEO Optimized**: Proper meta tags, semantic HTML, and optimized structure
- **Accessible**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant with keyboard navigation and screen reader support

## Pages

1. **Home (index.html)**: Hero section, services overview, featured conferences, testimonials
2. **About Us (about.html)**: Company history, mission, values, and team information
3. **Services (services.html)**: Detailed description of all conference management services
4. **Our Work (portfolio.html)**: Portfolio of managed conferences with case studies
5. **Resources (resources.html)**: Conference planning guides, best practices, and FAQs
6. **Contact (contact.html)**: Contact information, form, map, and office hours
