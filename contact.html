Request for Proposal (RFP)

Have a detailed RFP document? We'd love to review it and provide a comprehensive proposal.

Submit Your RFP

Please email your RFP document to info@pmmiconferences.com with the subject line "RFP Submission - [Your Conference Name]"

Include the following information:

  • Conference name and dates
  • Expected number of attendees
  • Location (if determined)
  • Scope of services needed
  • Budget range
  • Timeline for decision
  • Key contact information

We will review your RFP and respond with a detailed proposal within 5-7 business days.
